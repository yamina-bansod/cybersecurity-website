Understanding Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks. These cyberattacks are usually aimed at accessing, changing, or destroying sensitive information; extorting money from users; or interrupting normal business processes.

The Three Pillars (CIA Triad)

  1. Confidentiality: Ensuring that data is accessible only to those authorized to have access.
  2. Integrity: Ensuring that information is accurate and has not been tampered with.
  3. Availability: Ensuring that authorized users have reliable access to data when they need it.

As technology evolves, so do the tactics of cybercriminals. Whether you are an individual, a small business, or a large corporation, you are a potential target.
