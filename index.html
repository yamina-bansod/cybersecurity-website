Introduction

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from digital attacks.

In today's digital world, cyber threats are increasing rapidly, making awareness very important.

About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity involves protecting computers, mobile devices, servers, and networks from unauthorized access.

It helps in maintaining privacy, data protection, and safe online transactions.

Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake emails or messages to steal personal information.

Malware

Harmful software designed to damage systems or steal data.

Hacking

Unauthorized access to systems and networks.

Safety Tips

  • Use strong passwords
  • Do not share personal information online
  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Keep software updated
  • Avoid suspicious links

Cyber Laws Awareness

Cyber laws help protect users from online crimes.

In India, cybercrime is handled under the IT Act 2000.

🛡️ Cyber Safety Checklist

Internet par surakshit rehne ke liye in baaton ka dhyan rakhein:

  • Strong Passwords: Har account ke liye alag aur lamba password rakhein.
  • Two-Factor (2FA): Apne accounts mein 2-Factor Authentication zaroor on karein.
  • Update Software: Apne phone aur apps ko hamesha update rakhein.
  • Public Wi-Fi: Banking ya private kaam ke liye public Wi-Fi ka use na karein.
  • Suspicious Links: Anjaan links ya attachments par click na karein.
