Cyber Laws in India (IT Act 2000)

Knowledge of laws helps you seek justice if you are targeted by cybercriminals.

  • Section 66: Hacking with computer systems is a punishable offense.
  • Section 66C: Identity theft (using someone else's password/signature).
  • Section 66D: Cheating by personation using computer resources.
How to Report?
If you are a victim, visit www.cybercrime.gov.in or call the National Helpline Number 1930.
